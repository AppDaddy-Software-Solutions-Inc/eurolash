Become a

Lash Artist in Kingston

With 1-1 Taining

One-on-One Eyelash Extension Taining is ideal for those with no prior lash experience or previously certified lash artists looking to increase their education, skill level, and earning potential. An ideal career opportunity for students or stay-at-home moms looking to generate extra income. THIS IS A 2-DAY COURSE THAT RUNS FROM 10 AM – 5 PM EACH DAY. 

 

The Price for 1:1 Training

$1500 plus tax including starter kit, training book and certificate.

Run Time

The classes will only run on Sundays.

1:1Training Deposit

There is $300 deposit in order to book your class and the remaining can be paid on the day of the class.

Once you complete

your first day

You then have to practice for a week and send over your practice photos for critique. After 1 week you come back with your live model and all the questions you may have. Once you do your first new set on your model we can go through hybrid technique.

After that you will be receiving your One-on-one Eyelash Extension Training certificate.
